# Captain Dashboard - User Guide

**Version:** 1.0
**Date:** February 5, 2026

---

## Overview

The Captain Dashboard is a dedicated interface for team captains to manage their team roster, review player join requests, and track team performance.

---

## Accessing Your Captain Dashboard

### From the Sidebar:

1. Log in to your account
2. Look for the **"My Teams"** section in the sidebar
3. Click on your team name
4. You'll be taken to your Captain Dashboard

**Note:** The "My Teams" section only appears if you are a team captain.

---

## Dashboard Features

### Overview Tab

The Overview tab provides a quick snapshot of your team:

**Quick Stats:**
- **Roster Size:** Current players vs maximum roster size
- **Pending Requests:** Number of players waiting for approval
- **Upcoming Games:** Next 5 scheduled games

**Upcoming Games List:**
- Game date and time
- Opponent information
- Venue location
- Home/Away indicator

**Quick Actions:**
- Jump to review pending requests
- Jump to manage roster

---

### Roster Tab

View and manage your team's roster:

**Features:**
- Search players by name or email
- View player contact information (email, phone)
- See jersey numbers
- See player positions
- Remove players from roster

**Player Information Displayed:**
- Jersey number
- Player name
- Position
- Email address
- Phone number (if provided)

**Actions:**
- **Remove Player:** Click the "Remove" button to remove a player from the roster
  - Confirmation required
  - Cannot remove yourself (the captain)

**Special Indicators:**
- Captain has a shield badge next to their name

---

### Join Requests Tab

Review and manage player join requests:

**Filter Options:**
- **Pending:** Requests waiting for your decision
- **Approved:** Previously approved requests
- **Rejected:** Previously rejected requests

**Request Information:**
- Player name
- Player email and phone
- Date/time of request
- Optional message from player

**Actions for Pending Requests:**

1. **Approve:**
   - Optionally enter a jersey number (0-99)
   - Click "Approve"
   - Player will be automatically added to the roster

2. **Reject:**
   - Click "Reject"
   - Confirm the rejection
   - Player will be notified (if notifications enabled)

**Tips:**
- Review the player's optional message for context
- Assign jersey numbers during approval to save time
- Check roster size before approving (see Overview tab)

---

### Schedule Tab

View your team's schedule:

**Features:**
- Shows upcoming games
- Links to full season schedule
- Displays game dates, times, and venues

**Note:** For complete schedule management, click "Go to Season Schedule"

---

### Statistics Tab

View team performance metrics:

**Current Stats:**
- Wins
- Losses
- Ties

**Note:** Statistics will be populated once games are played and scored.

## Common Tasks

### Approving a Join Request

1. Go to the **Join Requests** tab
2. Ensure **Pending** filter is selected
3. Review player information
4. (Optional) Enter jersey number in the "Jersey #" field
5. Click **Approve**
6. Player is automatically added to your roster

### Removing a Player

1. Go to the **Roster** tab
2. Find the player in the list (use search if needed)
3. Click **Remove** button next to the player
4. Confirm the removal
5. Player is removed from the roster

### Viewing Team Stats

1. Go to the **Overview** tab for quick stats
2. Or go to the **Statistics** tab for detailed performance
3. View wins, losses, and ties
4. Check upcoming games schedule

### Finding Contact Info

1. Go to the **Roster** tab
2. Search for the player if needed
3. View email and phone in the Contact column
4. Click to copy or use your mail app

## Tips & Best Practices

### Managing Join Requests:

- **Check Regularly:** Look for the badge indicator in the sidebar
- **Review Messages:** Players may provide important context in their message
- **Assign Jersey Numbers:** Do it during approval to avoid doing it later
- **Check Roster Size:** Ensure you have space before approving

### Roster Management:

- **Keep Contact Info Updated:** Make sure you can reach your players
- **Assign Positions:** Helps with game planning (future feature)
- **Remove Inactive Players:** Keep roster current for scheduling

### Communication:

- **Use Contact Info:** Reach out to players via email or phone
- **Explain Rejections:** If you need to reject a request, consider reaching out
- **Team Meetings:** Use roster to send group emails (future feature)

## Troubleshooting

### "Access Denied" Message

**Problem:** You see an "Access Denied" message when trying to access a captain dashboard.

**Solution:**
- Verify you are the team captain
- Contact league admin to assign you as captain
- Check that you're logged into the correct account

### Can't See "My Teams" in Sidebar

**Problem:** The "My Teams" section doesn't appear in the sidebar.

**Solution:**
- You may not be a team captain yet
- Contact league admin to be assigned as captain
- Check that your team has been created in the league

### Join Requests Not Appearing

**Problem:** You know there are join requests but they're not showing.

**Solution:**
- Check the filter (Pending/Approved/Rejected)
- Refresh the page
- Contact league admin if issue persists

### Cannot Remove Player

**Problem:** The "Remove" button is missing or disabled.

**Solution:**
- You cannot remove yourself (the captain)
- Ensure the player is still active on the roster
- Contact league admin if you need to remove the captain

### Roster Full

**Problem:** You want to approve a request but roster is full.

**Solution:**
- Check current roster size on Overview tab
- Remove inactive players to make space
- Contact league admin to increase max roster size

## Privacy & Security

### Your Responsibilities:

- **Protect Player Info:** Don't share player contact information publicly
- **Secure Your Account:** Use a strong password, enable 2FA

## FAQ

**Q: Can I have multiple teams?**
A: Yes! If you captain multiple teams, they'll all appear in the "My Teams" section.

**Q: Can I assign someone else as captain?**
A: Contact your league admin to change team captain.

**Q: What happens when I approve a join request?**
A: The player is automatically added to your roster and can participate in games.

**Q: Can I undo an approval or rejection?**
A: Contact your league admin if you need to reverse a decision.

**Q: How do I change team colors or logo?**
A: Use the team settings page (link in the dashboard).

**Q: Can I export the roster?**
A: Export feature coming soon. For now, you can copy/paste from the roster table.

**Q: What if a player doesn't show up for games?**
A: Use the "Remove" function to remove them from your roster.

**Q: Can I message players through the dashboard?**
A: Messaging feature coming soon. For now, use the contact info provided.
