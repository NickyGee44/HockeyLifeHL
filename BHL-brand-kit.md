# Beer League Hockey (BLH) Brand Kit — *Pond Hockey SaaS*

## 1. Brand Vision & Strategy
**Beer League Hockey** is transitioning to a "Pond Hockey" aesthetic. This signals pure, authentic hockey—community over hierarchy, skill over status. It resolves the tension between "beer" and "SaaS" by providing a professional, nostalgic, and honest platform.

*   **SaaS Front Door:** [beerleaguehockey.ca](https://beerleaguehockey.ca)
*   **Brand Role:** Infrastructure on the ice—not the referee.
*   **Vibe:** Outdoor pond hockey, frozen lakes, skated ice.

---

## 2. Brand Positioning

### What It Is
The authentic infrastructure for adult hockey. A platform that feels like real hockey because it's built on the fundamentals of the game.

### Why Pond Hockey?
*   **Universal:** Appeals to all ages and skill levels.
*   **Authentic:** Pre-commercial hockey roots.
*   **Universal Legibility:** Internationally recognized symbols of the game (rink lines, ice texture).

### Positioning Statement
> **Beer League Hockey** is the modern platform for running adult hockey leagues — drafts, stats, payments, and league management in one place. Built for the leagues we actually play in.

## 4. Visual Identity (The "Skated Ice" Canvas)

### UI Layering Rule
1.  **Ice Texture (Background):** Skated pond ice texture, ambient, blurred (12-24px), low contrast (4-8% opacity).
2.  **White Content Cards:** Clean, off-white containers for all data.
3.  **Content/Actions:** High-contrast text and primary blue actions.

### Core Color Palette (Rink Geometry)

| Token | Hex | Purpose |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Ice White** | `#F7FAFC` | Main background & page surface. |
| **Rink Blue** | `#1F4FD8` | **Action color.** Buttons, links, primary UI. |
| **Goal Red** | `#D72638` | **Discipline/Danger only.** Penalties, alerts, delete actions. |
| **Midnight** | `#0B1220` | Primary text and headings. |
| **Slate** | `#7B8794` | Secondary text, meta-data. |
| **Frost Grey** | `#E6EBF2` | Borders, subtle dividers, disabled states. |

---

## 5. Rink Geometry as Design Language
Instead of heavy logos, use rink markings as UI atoms:
*   **Dividers:** Thin blue lines (1-2px, 20% opacity).
*   **Active States:** Short blue-line underlines.
*   **Loading:** Dotted faceoff circles or rotating pucks.
*   **Separators:** Hashmark ticks for stat columns.

## 8. Brand Rules (Protect the Ice)

### Do
*   ✅ **Restraint:** Keep texture subtle to maintain readability.
*   ✅ **Neutrality:** Let league jerseys (colors) pop against the ice (platform).
*   ✅ **Function First:** Prioritize data clarity over stylistic flourishes.

### Don't
*   ❌ **No Cosplay:** Avoid "cracked ice" or "shattered glass" clichés.
*   ❌ **Spare the Red:** Never use Goal Red for decoration; it signifies an alert.
*   ❌ **No Slang:** Use professional terms (GP, PIM, GAA) instead of locker room jargon.
